The Series

For the girls who came to race.

Beat The Boys is a global race series built for women who line up to compete, not to make up the numbers. The brief is simple: beat more boys than you did last time.

Every race is a closed-road, full-send night out. A squad in matching bibs, a real start line, real results, and a party at the finish. We'll tell you exactly how many boys you beat. Then we go again.

by Mary McCarths x
